BEHAVIOUR
- Shared crm_pickup_reminders table, authenticated read with RLS.
- Manual deadline override RPC: crm_set_pickup_reminder_deadline.
- Confirmation RPC: crm_confirm_pickup_reminder (first / last).
- Service-role auto RPC: crm_sync_pickup_reminder_auto; preserves a manually confirmed deadline for the same TTN, resets stage acknowledgements if deadline or TTN changes.
- Carrier tracking adapters provide trackingPickupArrivedAt based on ready-for-pickup/branch-arrival carrier event, never trackingExpectedDeliveryAt.
- Tracking worker derives a pickup deadline only when rule inputs are present, persists it for Android, and uses crm_delivery_tracking_state.details.pickupReminderKey to avoid repeating RPC on unchanged tracking polls.

CARRIER POLICY LIMITS — FAIL CLOSED
- NOVA POSHTA: 5 calendar storage days after branch arrival + auto-return on day 6, explicitly reported by SpecMarket owner; effective LAST pickup day = arrival date + 5 days. Extension/custom policy not retrievable from current API; use per-TTN manual override when extended. The website's generic 5 free days is NOT universal auto return.
  Official: https://novaposhta.ua/additional-services/auto-return/
- ROZETKA DELIVERY: 10-day hold on arrival at ROZETKA store, inferred ONLY for final_department metadata and verified branch-arrival event; check exact per-parcel expiry in carrier UI when extending. Arrival counted as day 1 (deadline arrival+9). Carrier help: https://help.rozetka.delivery/
- MEEST: branch category matters. APT/<=10 kg auto-returns after 5 working days; warehouse/<=30 kg may allow paid storage until 10 working days. Pure rule engine supports both but current Meest status API adapter DOES NOT identify branch category; no auto deadline is generated unless trackingPickupLocationType is provided by trusted tracking metadata. Manual date can be supplied.
  Official: https://meestposhta.com.ua/storage/pdf/standartni_umovi_servisu.pdf
  https://meestposhta.com.ua/parcels-in-ukraine
- UKRPOSHTA: 7 days (urban) / 14 days (rural) are FREE storage periods, not proof of auto return. Not converted into auto deadline. A trustworthy carrier-specific last-pickup date is necessary for auto (trackingVerifiedLastPickupAt); otherwise manual.
  Official: https://www.ukrposhta.ua/ua/manage-parcels

IMPORTANT: The carrier adapters do not currently populate trackingVerifiedLastPickupAt or Meest pickupLocationType; these are safe future extension points, not active promises.
This patch does NOT provide automatic deadlines for every Ukrposhta/Meest parcel and must not be described as complete four-carrier automation.
No existing orders should receive invented final-day prompts.
