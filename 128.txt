Simple algorithm
For each day:
1. Take authoritative day closing balance.
2. Sum all valid known account movements of that day:
   credit = +amount
   debit = -amount
3. If stated opening is present AND reconciles exactly with closing, use it.
4. Otherwise derive opening:
   opening = closing - net day movements
5. Sort valid movements by occurredAt ascending.
6. Walk forward:
   credit => add
   debit => subtract
7. Store current balance after incoming movements.

Important
- Unknown/non-account conducted documents are ignored.
- Malformed known movements are skipped; they must not erase valid balances for the whole day.
- The calculation is anchored to the authoritative closing balance.
- Existing forward running-balance behavior stays intact.
- No reverse per-receipt algorithm is introduced.

derived opening = 8000.43 - 752.22 = 7248.21

Then forward:
7248.21 + 125.37 = 7373.58
7373.58 + 626.85 = 8000.43
