- Click day, month or year in an existing date: the whole clicked component becomes selected, so typing new digits replaces the existing digits WITHOUT Backspace/Delete.
- Example: in 23.09.2026 click the day and type 25 -> 25.09.2026; click month and type 12 -> 25.12.2026; click year and type 2027 -> 25.12.2027.
- Keyboard Tab into a valid date field selects the day so direct typing works immediately.
